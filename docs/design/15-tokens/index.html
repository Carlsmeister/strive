Colour

All 48 roles of Material 3's ColorScheme in four schemes. Light and dark follow the phone; the two + schemes are Increase Contrast. Leaving a role out would let Compose fill it with Material's default purple.

Contrast

Every pair that carries text, computed by tokens.py, which refuses to write the tokens if one fails. Text needs 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) and 7:1 under Increase Contrast (AAA); borders need 3:1. Text on glass depends on what is behind it, so glass thickens under Increase Contrast instead of getting a number.

Type

Apple's text styles under Material 3's names, in the system face: SF Pro on iPhone, Roboto on Android. Each row in English and Swedish, since Swedish runs longer and writes 82,5 where English writes 82.5. Numbers always use tabular figures.

Space, corners and glass

Space

Steps of 4 points. Material has no spacing tokens, so these are Strive's own.

Corners

Material's shape names with iOS-like values. Cards and buttons are medium or large, sheets extra large, capsules full.

Glass

Regular for bars and labels, thick for rows inside a sheet. Blur 24 points. There is no elevation scale: layers come from the surface roles, and only glass casts a shadow.

Compose mapping

What strive#3 writes for each token. Colour, type and shape go into MaterialTheme; space and glass are Strive's own, because Material has no slot for them.

Apple HIG review

Run on this page with apple-hig-review on 2026-10-20. Every rule quoted below comes from a HIG page read that day; the rest is marked judgment.

  • Contrast meets Apple's minimum. Accessibility: 4.5:1 for text up to 17 pt. Every text pair passes in light and dark, and 7:1 under Increase Contrast. Dark Mode adds: "For custom foreground and background colors, strive for a contrast ratio of 7:1, especially in small text." Standard schemes do not reach that; listed as a deviation.
  • Increase Contrast is covered. Color: "If you define a custom color, make sure to supply light and dark variants, and an increased contrast option for each variant." The two + schemes are that option.
  • Custom colours instead of system colours. Color: "Avoid hard-coding system color values in your app." Strive's greys copy Apple's system greys as fixed values, because Compose draws its own colours. Listed as a deviation.
  • Text sizes are Apple's. Typography: body 17 pt, minimum 11 pt; the smallest token, label-small, is 11. Larger Text is not supported yet: Accessibility asks for "at least 200 percent". Compose Multiplatform 1.12 does not scale text with the iPhone setting; 1.13 (in alpha) adds it. A requirement for strive#3, not a token.
  • The app follows the phone's appearance. Dark Mode: "Avoid offering an app-specific appearance setting." This matches Q6, and strive#14's Settings screen no longer lists an appearance setting (Carl, 2026-10-20).
  • Glass is used sparingly, on the right layer, with one exception. Materials: Liquid Glass is "a distinct functional layer for controls and navigation elements — like tab bars", and "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer." The tab bar and sheets follow it; the label on the run map does not and is listed below. Tab bars: "A tab bar floats above content", which the preview does.
  • For strive#16 (judgment): text links need a 44 × 44 pt tap area (Accessibility: default 44 × 44, minimum 28 × 28), while "Set a target" is only 22 pt tall. Sheets in dark mode take surface-container-high, Apple's elevated background (Dark Mode: base and elevated backgrounds). Primary teal marks what can be tapped and the selected state, never plain text (Color: "Avoid using the same color to mean different things").

Sources opened: accessibility, color, dark-mode, typography, tab-bars, materials.

Decided

  • Direction B: calm, clear, native. Grey grouped background, white cards, one teal tint, regular weights (Carl, 2026-10-20, Q2).
  • Glass on controls and overlays (G2): the tab bar, sheets such as Start, and labels laid over maps and images. Cards and lists stay solid. With Reduce Transparency on, the app shows the solid surface instead; strive#3 reads the setting and picks the blur library (Haze is the candidate: iOS and Android, Apache-2.0, a translucent fill without blur on Android 11 and older) (Carl, 2026-10-20, Q3).
  • Apple's text styles under Material 3's names: body is 17/22, the screen title 34/41, captions 11–13 (Carl, 2026-10-20, Q4).
  • Numbers in the system face (N1), SF Pro on iPhone and Roboto on Android, always with tabular figures (Carl, 2026-10-20, Q5).
  • Every one of the 48 colour roles has a value in every scheme; Increase Contrast is two schemes of its own, aiming for 7:1; the app follows the phone's light or dark setting; space in steps of 4; corners as listed; no elevation scale, layers come from the surface roles and glass; every later prototype is checked for hard-coded values in strive#20 (Carl, 2026-10-20, Q6).
  • Token names follow Material 3's roles for colour, type and shape (--color-on-primary is colorScheme.onPrimary), plus a spacing scale of Strive's own, which Material does not have (Carl, 2026-10-20, Q1).

Deviations from Apple's guidelines

Accepted for now, because Carl is the only user. Listed so they are cheap to fix if Strive is ever submitted to the App Store.

  • Fixed colour values instead of Apple's system colours. Apple: "Avoid hard-coding system color values in your app." Compose draws its own colours, so Strive copies Apple's greys as fixed values and will not follow Apple's future adjustments. The fix on iPhone is reading UIKit's dynamic colours in strive#3.
  • 4.5:1, not 7:1, in the standard schemes. Apple: "For custom foreground and background colors, strive for a contrast ratio of 7:1, especially in small text." Strive meets 7:1 only under Increase Contrast. The fix is the + schemes' values everywhere, at the cost of a darker, heavier look.
  • Glass labels on maps and images (G2). Apple: "Don't use Liquid Glass in the content layer." A label laid over a run map sits in that layer. The fix is a solid label (G1's map).

Considered

The sketches each decision was made from, kept as the record of why. Their values are sketches, not tokens. The switch sets every sketch phone.

Considered: numbers (N1 chosen)

The active workout, where numbers matter most: the session clock, each set, the rest timer. The same screen in three number faces. Rounded needs an iPhone or a Mac; other browsers show the default face.

N1 · System, like Apple's own apps

SF Pro on iPhone, Roboto on Android. Tabular figures so the clock does not jump.

N2 · Rounded, like Apple Fitness

SF Pro Rounded on iPhone. Android has no SF; it needs a rounded face of its own (an open licence font, bundled).

N3 · Monospaced, like the old Strive

SF Mono on iPhone. The old app's Type.kt used monospace for numbers. Wider, so long values crowd sooner.

Considered: where glass goes (G2 chosen)

Direction B with Apple-style glass in three amounts. Scroll each phone: the run map passes under the tab bar. The app falls back to the solid surface when Reduce Transparency is on; Safari does not report that setting, so this page cannot show it.

G1 · Controls only

The tab bar floats as glass. Cards and lists stay solid. Apple's own rule for glass in iOS 26.

G2 · Controls and overlays

As G1, plus sheets and labels laid over content: the Start sheet, the label on the run map.

G3 · Everywhere

Cards are glass too, over a tinted background. The most glass, and the least calm.

Considered: visual direction (B chosen)

The three directions B was chosen from, with each one's contrast.

A · Energetic, bold, loud

The old Strive: orange on black, heavy titles and numbers. Orange is a fill with black text on it, never text on white.

B · Calm, clear, native

Looks like an Apple app: grey grouped background, white cards, one teal tint, regular weights.

C · Focused, precise, technical

A training instrument: near monochrome, hairline borders, small corners, cobalt for action, monospaced numbers.
