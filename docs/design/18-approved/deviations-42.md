that owns it. Shots come from `WorkoutShotTest` into `/tmp/strive-board-shots`; `python3 tools/compare.py <ID>` renders the board
beside them. dark-HC is compared first, then dark. The pull request quotes this list.

**Remaining differences per board**, after Task 13 compared all 40 boards and the two motion boards, and hardening re-measured the
40 against the d8f8fc9 shots (the Log check against 9e8215a's), dark-HC then dark. A board counts lines, not elements: one for
each line below that names it. A line holds one element, or neighbouring elements with one cause (the focus label and values); the
Plan note and the plates line, which most boards miss, have a line each. Lines owned "Not a difference" and lines whose board is
"None" are not counted. A difference under 1 pt is not listed. Only one line shows in dark alone, the glass's teal glow; every
