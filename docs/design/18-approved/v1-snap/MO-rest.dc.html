The rest timer ending

Seconds left on the card. The digits never animate; only the ring and the colour do.

Counting

The ring drains in 1 s linear ticks (motion-tick). − and + change the total by 15 s; the ring springs to its new share (release spring 350). Skip ends the rest at once: the card fades (250) without the buzz.

Ending

At 3 s the ring and digits cross-fade to teal (250). At 0: a sound and a long buzz, the ring closes teal and the label reads "Rest over · Set 3 next", held 600 ms, then the card settles 8 pt and fades (250). The list never scrolls by itself.

Elsewhere
