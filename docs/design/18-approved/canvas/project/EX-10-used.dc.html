Instructions

  1. Lie with your eyes under the bar, shoulder blades squeezed together and down.
  2. Grip a little wider than your shoulders, thumbs around the bar.
  3. Lower the bar under control to the lower part of your chest.
  4. Press up and slightly back until your arms are straight.

Tips

Keep your feet planted and drive through the floor.

Stack your wrists over your elbows at the bottom.
