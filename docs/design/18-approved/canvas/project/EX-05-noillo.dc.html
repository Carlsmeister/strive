Swap: the alternative, Single Row DB, is suggested first.

Instructions

  1. Sit tall with your feet on the platform and your knees slightly bent.
  2. Take the handle with straight arms, shoulders down.
  3. Pull the handle to your lower ribs, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  4. Return slowly until your arms are straight.

Tips

Keep your torso still; do not lean back to finish the rep.

Lead with the elbows, not the hands.
