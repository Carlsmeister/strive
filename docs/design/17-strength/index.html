Decided

Every screen's content is decided in the grilling on strive#17, Q1–Q47. The rules every sketch follows: tokens only; components from 16; Strong is inspiration for functions, never for looks; teal only for what can be tapped or is selected; glass only on controls and overlays; one set logged in three taps at most.

Deviations from Apple's guidelines

Accepted for now, because Carl is the only user. Listed so they are cheap to fix if Strive is ever submitted to the App Store.

  • RIR 4 or more is chosen from a row of 4–10 that the 4+ segment opens in the dock; the segment then shows the value (Carl, 2026-10-24). An RIR of 4 or more therefore costs one tap more, and joins Q13's listed exceptions. Not a deviation; listed here so the cost is visible.
  • Session RPE is a segmented row of ten on the summary (Q28). Apple, Segmented controls: on iPhone, keep to about five segments. Each segment is about 34 pt wide and stays 44 pt tall. The fix is a picker or a 1–10 slider, which costs a tap or a drag more.
  • RIR segments are about 29 pt wide in the dock, because RIR shares its row with the rest time (Carl, 2026-10-24). Apple, Accessibility: a hit target of 44 × 44 pt by default, 28 × 28 pt at minimum. They stay 44 pt tall and above the minimum. The fix is RIR on a row of its own, full width, which squeezes the rest time again.
